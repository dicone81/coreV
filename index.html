Glossary
RT (T30)
Reverberation time: how long a sound takes to fade away (by 60 dB) after it stops. Longer means more reverberant. Piano 1.5–2.4 s, installation 3–4 s.
EDT
Early decay time: the same idea, but from only the first part of the fade. It's what you perceive as reverberance while music is playing. Ideally close to the RT.
C80 (worst)
Clarity: the sound arriving in the first 80 ms versus everything after, in dB. Higher means notes stay distinct; lower means they blend. "Worst" is the lower of the two pit listeners (L1, L2). Piano: −3 dB or higher.
Double slope
How much slower the late decay is than the early decay. 1.3× or more is an audible "bloom" from the upper room.
Bass ratio
Reverb at 125 Hz divided by reverb at 500 Hz. 1.0–1.2 is balanced; higher means the bass booms on longer than the mids.
L2 deepest drop
How much quieter L2 is than the rest of the pit in its quietest bass band (wave simulation). This is the quiet zone below the neck.
